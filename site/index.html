Snap the price tag. Watch your budget.

yaga is a grocery budget app for the supermarket aisle. Photograph an item with its price tag and it joins your cart as a sticker, sized by what it costs. What's left of your budget stays in view as peso bills and coins.

Follow the build on GitHub

The Android app is in development. No sign-up, no ads.

Tap an item to put it in the cart. Tap again to take it out.

Built for one hand in a busy aisle

  1. Snap the item with its price tag

    One photo reads the barcode and the shelf price. You check the number before it counts.

  2. It joins your cart as a sticker

    A bigger price makes a bigger sticker, so the expensive things stand out at a glance.

  3. Your budget shows as cash

    See the bills and coins you have left, or an IOU slip with the amount once you go over.

Works with no signal

Everything is saved on your phone first, so the freezer aisle with zero bars is fine. Syncing to the web dashboard is optional.

Pesos first

Prices in pesos and centavos, a budget per run, and receipts checked against the shelf prices you snapped.

yaga is short for YetAnotherGroceryApp. Source and progress on GitHub.
