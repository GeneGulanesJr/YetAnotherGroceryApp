Core Principle

The mobile application is designed to capture grocery purchases with minimal effort during shopping while verifying checkout accuracy through receipt analysis. The shopping list helps users prepare for future trips using their personal purchase history and estimated pricing. The desktop application transforms accumulated purchase history into actionable insights about spending, pricing, shopping habits, savings, and long-term trends.

The system becomes more valuable over time as it builds a rich personal history of purchases, prices, shopping behavior, and store comparisons, enabling users to make smarter grocery purchasing decisions while continuously improving future shopping trips.
